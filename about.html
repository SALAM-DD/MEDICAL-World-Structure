Our Origin Story

From a Student Initiative to a Global Ecosystem

Founded in 2018 by Abdulazeez F. O., Medical World began as a peer study circle designed to help medical students tackle tough professional examinations. Over the past 8 years, it has grown into an international platform serving over 10,000 members across universities worldwide.

Today, we are rebuilding the platform from the ground up to incorporate advanced AI study assistants, interactive clinical simulations, and real-time national leaderboard leagues.
